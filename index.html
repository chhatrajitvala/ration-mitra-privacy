Ration Mitra – Privacy Policy

Incredible View Studio · Effective date: 6 November 2026

Englishગુજરાતીहिन्दी

English

Ration Mitra ("the app") is developed by Incredible View Studio. It helps fair price shop owners in Gujarat keep track of monthly ration distribution. This policy explains what information the app handles and how. In short: all data stays on your phone, and we do not collect, store or share any of it.

1. Information stored on your phone

The app stores the following only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • Ration card lists (G-register) that you download from ipds.gujarat.gov.in or add as PDF: card number, category, card holder and head of family names, number of members, address and village.
  • Sale reports that you fetch or upload: card numbers, transaction dates and quantities.
  • Mobile numbers you type in for card holders, and cards you mark by hand.
  • Your settings: language, chosen district, taluka and shop(s).

This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. We do not run any server and have no access to it.

2. Connection to ipds.gujarat.gov.in

When you ask it to, the app opens public pages of the website ipds.gujarat.gov.in, the same pages you can open in a browser. It sends only what is needed to open those pages: the month, district, taluka, shop or village you choose, and the captcha text you type. No information about you or your phone is added.

3. Payments

The app and its subscriptions are sold through Google Play. Payments are processed entirely by Google; the app never sees your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether your subscriptions are active. Google's handling of this information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Backup

If Android backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include the app's data in your personal Google account backup, so it can be restored on a new phone. This backup is controlled by you in your phone's settings and is not accessible to us.

5. What we do not do

  • No account or login, no advertising, no analytics or tracking.
  • No access to contacts, location, camera, microphone or phone calls. "Call" only opens your phone's dialer with the number.
  • Files are read only when you pick them; sharing (WhatsApp, PDF) happens only when you tap Share.

6. Deleting your data

You can delete G-register lists and undo sale uploads inside the app. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android settings, removes everything from the phone.

7. Children

The app is meant for shop owners and is not directed at children under 18.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

9. Contact

Incredible View Studio – questions about this policy: incredibleviewstudios@gmail.com

10. Disclaimer

Ration Mitra is not a government app and is not affiliated with the Government of Gujarat or the Food & Civil Supplies Department. Ration card and sale data is read from the public website ipds.gujarat.gov.in.

  • आपके द्वारा लाई या अपलोड की गई बिक्री रिपोर्ट: कार्ड नंबर, लेन-देन की तारीख और मात्रा।

यह जानकारी कभी हमें या किसी और को नहीं भेजी जाती। हमारा कोई सर्वर नहीं है और हमें इसकी कोई पहुँच नहीं है।
